Media literacy



How can you explore without understanding?



Without understanding and good guiding principles, exploring anything becomes an exhausting task. You as students may have found that the internet can be a devisive place full of different ideas and intentions. You may at times have felt lost or our of your depth. Our aim is to introduce A strong framework and possiblities to saftily traverse the internet with a sense of control.

What will you learn?

  • The digital landscape
  • Ability to spot fake news
  • Media strategies
  • Understanding of Algorithmic biases.


Where?

Groningen, Hamonie complex

When?

The 4th and 5th of July (Friday & Saturday)

Cost

The course will cost 120 euro's, will consist of 2 days and include some drinks and food.
