Recipes

Cupcake recipes

For about 12 cupcakes you will need:

  • 120ml of yoghurt
  • 260g sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 120g butter
  • 75g flour
  • 1 bag of baking powder
  • pinch of salt
  • Vanilla essence

First mix the yogurt, sugar and eggs together. You can use a mixer or with a ladle. The butter can melt in a microwave or a saucepan and then mixed with the other ingredients. Then add the flower, baking powder, salt and a dash of vanilla essence. This is the batter for the cupcakes! Mix it up a little and then you are ready to divide the batter.

Dividing the batter over the molds can be done with spoons but you can also use a scoop. The molds should be filled up to about three quarters. You can put the molds in the oven but you can also put these in a so-called muffin tin so they remain in shape. After 25 minutes at 180 degrees they are ready, provided they have a natural golden brown crust and are cooked inside. This can be tested by pricking with a skewer into the cake. Does the skewer comes out dry? Then the cakes are done! Before the topping is put on the cupcakes, these have to cool down first. Preferably in the oven but it also goes well when you put a baking dish over it.

For the toppings, you need:

  • 100g butter
  • 250g icing sugar
  • Jam
  • Cream cheese (e.g. Philadelphia natural)
  • Ornaments

Put the butter, cream cheese and powdered sugar in a mixing bowl. Do not directly use the mixer for the powder will spurt across the kitchen. First knead it with your (warm) hands once or twice so it becomes less watery and then you can use the mixer. Then add (for taste) the jam. About 3 tablespoons but that depends on what kind of jam you use and what you prefer. Mix it again and then add the topping into a pastry bag so you can make beautiful tufts. Once the tufts are put on the cupcakes, you can decorate it further, for example with a disco dip, chocolate, strawberries or anything else you like.

Bon appetit!

------ Additional recipe, chocolate cupcakes ----

about 12 chocolate cupcakes you will need:

  • 125g butter
  • 125 grams of granulated sugar
  • 135g self-raising flour
  • 2 tablespoons of milk, at room temperature
  • 2 eggs, at room temperature
  • 4 tablespoons cocoa powder (at least 70% cocoa per 100 grams)

If you make this chocolate cupcakes: Mix the butter and sugar together well until you get a fluffy substance. Then you can add the eggs one by one. High speed mixing helps to make a very light batter. After that you can add the flour, little by little, while mixing. Finally, add a little milk, mix it again, and finally add 4 tablespoons cocoa powder mixed with 4 tablespoons of hot water. Divide this mix over all the molds. Then put these in the oven for about 20 minutes at 170 degrees Celsius.
