Bakery

How are these cupcakes made?

Baking
To start, the necessary ingredients are carefully weighed and gradually mixed together. If the batter is ready, we put paper cases in a special cupcake / cake / muffin tin so they remain in shape. Then we pour about three quarters of the batter with two spoons in the molds. Just half an hour in the oven and they're ready!

Decorating
After being cooled, put the cupcakes, in the oven or under, for example, an oven dish so that they gradually cool down and do not collapse, are decorated. This can be done by means of a topping. A mix of powdered sugar, butter, cream cheese and jam. This topping then goes into a piping bag with which tufts can be made on top of the cupcakes. When the cakes are provided with a topping of your choice, you can add fruit such as strawberries or for example other decorations like chocolate or disco dip for a colorful effect.
