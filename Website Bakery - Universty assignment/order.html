How To Order

Deserts that will make you think
of home

At Jenny & Tammy's Bakery, we specialize in crafting custom cupcakes for every special occasion — from birthdays and weddings to parties and anniversaries. As a small, part-time mother-daughter business, each batch is made with care, creativity, and a personal touch.

To order, just send us an email with what you have in mind — flavors, colors, themes, quantity, and date — for you're tailor made deserts — and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Email: Jenny&TammyBakery@gmail.com

We can't wait to bake something just for you!
