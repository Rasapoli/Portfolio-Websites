Jenny & Tammy's Cake's

Not just cupcakes

On occasion you may not want cupcakes (however, good they may taste), and we understand. That's why we bake cakes too

We can make cakes for any occasion, be it a birthday, wedding or just because you want one. We can make them in any flavour you like, and we can even make them gluten free!

We also do custom cakes, so if you have a specific design in mind, let us know and we will do our best to make it happen.

For more information on our cakes, please contact us.
