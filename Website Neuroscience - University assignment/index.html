The Diving Reflex

Taking a cold plunge

The first thing most people might expect to happen when diving into cold water is their body becoming more active. After all, it is a situation most people would not want to be in, and getting out of the water requires activity. However, when you dive into cold water, something unexpected happens: the body reacts by becoming less and more active at the same time. Does the human body stress out when submerged in cold water or is something happening that's not visible on the surface?
