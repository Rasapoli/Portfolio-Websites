Experiment

So, what's the goal?

The goal of the experiment is to measure the physiological function of the autonomic nervous system, focusing on both the baroreceptor and the diving reflex. The effects of the reflexes on heart rate and blood pressure were assessed with a Biopac system. For those interested in observing their own autonomic responses, a simplified version of this experiment can be replicated at home using a heart rate monitor. Materials required

  1. A large bowl of water (sufficient to submerge the face)
  2. A snorkel
  3. A heart rate monitoring system (Biopac was used in this study)
  4. Diving goggles (optional)
  5. A mat (for participant comfort during positional changes)

Electrocardiogram (ECG) Measurement

The electrocardiogram measures the echo of communication toward the heart muscles. It works by picking up the electrical signals that arise from the heart during depolarization and repolarization. Utilizing sensitive sensors, the Biopac system detects this electrical activity. It is important to keep the body relatively still during the ECG, as other muscles produce their own electrical signals, which can interfere with the measurements.

Explanation of the experiment

Two experiments were performed. One experiment focused on the baroreceptor reflex and the other on the diving reflex. The sample consisted of 30 students for the baroreceptor reflex and 29 students for the diving reflex. The participants were not randomly selected; the sampling consisted of convenience sampling. This approach was chosen as the experiment did not aim to be highly generalizable but to give the students an insight into the physiological response of the autonomic nervous system. The sample consisted of both male and female students enrolled in the neuroscience minor, all aged between 20 and 30.
